About Us

An established pharmaceutical & healthcare products provider

Asad Pharmacy is located opposite Jinnah Hospital, Lahore, and has been part of the local healthcare landscape since 1995.

Our Story

Founded in 1995, opposite Jinnah Hospital

Asad Pharmacy was established in 1995 and has operated from Shop #2, Maulana Shaukat Ali Road, Al Rahim Homes, Lahore — directly opposite Jinnah Hospital. Over the years, the pharmacy has focused on serving patients, families and healthcare providers in the surrounding area.

Our published business profile centers on pharmaceutical products, prescription medicines, oncology and specialty medicines, surgical products, medical products and laboratory services.
